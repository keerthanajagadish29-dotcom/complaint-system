Supported Complaint Categories

Easily classify your concerns for fast-track resolution by specific domain experts

Technical Issues

Software bugs, portal access failures, network connectivity, hardware glitches, and login errors.

Academic Issues

Course materials, examination schedules, marksheets, syllabus clarifications, and faculty assistance.

Infrastructure Issues

Classroom facilities, lab equipment, electrical fittings, air conditioning, and physical maintenance.

Service Issues

Library services, hostel facilities, cafeteria quality, transportation, and administrative support services.

Other General Concerns

Any other grievances or suggestions not explicitly covered under standard operational categories.

How The System Works

3 simple steps to lodge and resolve grievances

1
Register & Submit

Create an account, select the category, set priority, and describe your issue with auto-generated Ticket ID.

2
Admin Processing

Administrators evaluate the ticket, update status (In Progress), assign priority, and take required actions.

3
Resolution & Tracking

Receive official admin remarks, track status updates to 'Resolved', and view detailed complaint logs anytime.
